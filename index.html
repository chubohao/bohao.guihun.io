About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at University Duisburg-Essen, Germany.

My research focuses on natural language processing, with an emphasis on evidence grounding, reliable language model reasoning, and medical text generation.

  • Evidence grounding and citation faithfulness
  • Medical summarization and question answering
  • Claim verification and language model evaluation
